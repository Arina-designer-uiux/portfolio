AI parking assistant  /  Mobile app

WAYLEADR

An AI concierge helps drivers find parking through chat, voice, and map-based results.

01  /  Overview

Parking searches start with a conversation.

I designed an AI concierge flow that turns a driver's parking request into tailored options. The assistant asks for key details—destination, time, vehicle size, and length of stay—then presents parking results to review.

Role
UX/UI design
Users
Drivers

Mobile app
Workflows
AI search · voice · quick booking

02  /  The challenge

Give AI enough detail to find a match.

The concierge gathers the destination, parking time, vehicle size, and length of stay through follow-up questions. It then summarizes the request and presents parking options with location, price, and a details action.

03  /  Key workflows

A conversation that leads to parking options.

  1. 01Describe your parking need

    Enter a destination and timing in chat, or use voice input.

  2. 02Refine the request

    Answer follow-up questions about time, vehicle size, and length of stay.

  3. 03Review parking options

    Compare suggested spaces and open details for the one that fits.

04  /  The solution

AI concierge, voice search, and Rush Mode.

The designs cover conversational parking search, voice input, and Rush Mode for finding nearby parking quickly. The app also presents an AI auto-booking option based on a user's parking habits.

Features / AI-assisted parking

  • VOICESpoken parking requests
  • RUSHQuick nearby parking
