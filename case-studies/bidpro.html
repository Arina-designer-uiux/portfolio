K–12 procurement  /  B2B platform

BIDPRO

Designing clear procurement workflows for school teams and vendors.

01  /  Overview

Procurement from both sides.

BidPro supports purchasing groups and districts across super admin, group, district, and school admin levels. Bid workflows involve bid admins, managers, team members, and viewers, while vendors use a separate portal to submit pricing, responses, and required documents.

Role
UX/UI design
Users

Workflows
IFB + RFP · setup to award

02  /  The challenge

Complex rules. Clear next steps.

A solicitation can involve setup rules, categories, questions, addenda, vendor uploads, evaluation, and award. Administrators need control and status visibility; vendors need to understand the requirements and finish every required step before submitting a bid.

03  /  Key workflows

Make every workflow visible.

  1. 01Solicitation setup

    Configure IFB or RFP solicitations, with optional market baskets, addenda, and category-level Q&A.

  2. 02Vendor submission

    Guide required answers and file uploads, allow partial saves, and show completion before bid submission.

  3. 03Review & response

    Support criteria-based RFP evaluation, category pricing, clear bid statuses, and a separate no-bid path.

04  /  The solution

From solicitation to award.

Connect admin setup, vendor responses, evaluation, and award in one guided system. Show required tasks, saved progress, category context, and status at the point each user needs them.
